MINA & MARIAN WEDDING INVITATION — NETLIFY UPLOAD

1) Upload
- Go to Netlify and use Add new site > Deploy manually.
- Drag this whole folder OR the ZIP file into the Netlify deploy area.
- Netlify will publish the invitation and give you a free netlify.app link.

2) RSVP form
- This site uses Netlify Forms (form name: wedding-rsvp).
- After the first deploy, Netlify detects the form automatically.
- RSVP submissions will appear in your Netlify dashboard under Forms.

3) Send RSVP notifications to Marian's email
- In Netlify open: Site configuration / Forms / Form notifications.
- Add an Email notification for new form submissions.
- Use: marianmories.h@gmail.com

4) Custom free site name (optional)
- In Netlify: Site configuration > Domain management > Options > Edit site name.
- Example: mina-marian-wedding.netlify.app (if available).

5) Main details already included
- Mina & Marian
- Wedding date: 10 October 2026
- Church: Saint Mary and Saint Beshoy Church, New Cairo — 6:00 PM
- Reception: Tolip El Narges Hotel & SPA — following the ceremony
- Countdown, location buttons, RSVP form, and Add to Calendar

IMPORTANT
The email notification destination is configured inside Netlify, not hard-coded in the public HTML. This keeps the email handling tied to your Netlify account and avoids relying on a third-party form service.
